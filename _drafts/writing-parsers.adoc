I am currently quite busy with architecture mapping in my day job and while
drawing and mapping is fun, I am sometimes a bit afraid of getting
rustyfootnote:[Pun absolutely intended..].
To fight this, I occasionally take a stroll through my projects and exhume
some older ones in my spare time.

Among my parked project is something I've creatively dubbed {spotql}[spotql],
a highly ambitious project to combine a {spotify}[Spotify] client and CLI of
{postgres}[Postgres] via {wireprot}[Postgres' wire protocol].

TIP: I am aware of the long list of really good libraries, like {pgwire}[pgwire],
but  I ask you where is the fun, if I cannot combine two completely unrelated
things  and invent the wheel again?

This whole project naturally involves writing a parser hence the name of
the blogpost you are currently reading.
So over the course of the next few lines I am going to delve a bit in how to
write parsers with {rust}[Rust], starting from the most naive version, over
to something you probably learn at the university to more or less
brainfuckfootnote:[The feeling, not the language, mind you!].
